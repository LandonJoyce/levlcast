## How attribution works

When someone uses your link (or types your code at checkout):

1. The discount applies automatically (they don't have to type anything if they click your link)
2. Their Stripe subscription is tagged with your `referral_code` metadata
3. Each month, I pull a report of active subscribers tagged with your code and pay you 30% of net revenue from those subs
4. Cancellations stop the payouts; renewals keep them going

Payouts go out monthly. First payout once you've had your first redemption clear Stripe's 7-day chargeback window.
